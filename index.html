01 / Fields of study

Three fields. One method.

Pick a question. Build the smallest thing that answers it. Publish what holds.

→ B

AI

What coding agents do when given real tools. Native software built with them, and instruments to watch them work.

→ C

What happens when the notes are published. Two podcasts, machine-written fiction, a daily digest, a course catalog.
